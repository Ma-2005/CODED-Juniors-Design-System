# Mascot

**Codey**, the CODED Juniors robot: a cream 3D robot with a black TV-screen face, cyan LED smile, red ear discs, a red chest button, a red cape and black gloves. One Codey per slide, big (500–800px on a 1920 slide), on the red side of hero and split slides.

**Pose by job (the camp-deck poses):**
- `codey-laptop-front.png`: program cover.
- `codey-hands-on-hips.png`: activity title.
- `codey-icebreaker-wall.png`: Ice Breaker (bursting through a wall; has its own light-blue scene, show it in a rounded 40px frame).
- `codey-running-cheer.png`: "You Are a [Role] Today!".
- `codey-thinking.png`: every question slide.
- `codey-idea-lightbulb.png`: Fun Fact and lightbulb moments.
- `codey-kneeling-ready.png`: Challenge Time.
- `codey-jumping-cheer.png`: Game Time and celebrations.
- `codey-lemonade-break.png`: Break Time.
- `codey-hello-bubble.png`: Show & Tell.
- `codey-reading-book.png`: What Did We Learn.
- `codey-waving.png`: Coming Up Next and hellos.
- `badge-ai-be-creative.png`, `badge-ai-responsibly.png`, `badge-ai-stay-safe.png`: the three "How to Use AI!" circle badges. Always together, always with their fixed text.

**Brand-guide versions:** `mascot-standing.png`, `mascot-jumping.png`, `mascot-thinking.png` and `mascot-cape-*.png` (hero, idea, laptop, sphero, standing, wave) come from the JR-25 Brand Guide and older decks; fine for print and social.

More poses (VR headset, 3D printer, Sphero, hard hat, hacker hoodie, AI visor, chef, soccer, astronaut…) are in the CODED Juniors Asset Library, folder 01 Codey Mascot. The Super Coder kids (boy and girl in red capes) exist only in the brand guide pages.

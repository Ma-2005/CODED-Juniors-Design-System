# Brand

CODED grounding devices and camp decoration.

- `bracket-pair-navy.png` / `bracket-pair-white.png`, `brackets-blue.png`: the CODED `[ ]` brackets, a quiet device around eyebrow labels on white slides.
- `pattern-dots.png`, `pattern-code.png`: subtle CODED patterns, allowed on white only.
- `echo-circle-c.png`: the white CODED "C" inside concentric blue echo rings on navy; `bg-navy-circle.jpg` is its plain navy ground.
- `speed-lines.png`, `shooting-star.png`: motion accents placed behind a moving mascot or icon.
- `question-circle-supercoder.png`: a blue disc with a red 3D "?", the Super Coder star and a وناسة tag, for question slides.
- `ball-mascot.png`: a smiling red-and-white volleyball character, for sports-camp materials.

# Decor

The 3D decoration that fills hero slides. Two to four pieces per red slide, around Codey and the title, never on top of text.

- `star-yellow-3d.png`: the glossy yellow star; tilted ±10–12° near titles and on dividers.
- `sparkle-white.png`: the white four-point sparkle; 48–90px, scattered in corners.
- `sparkles-yellow.png`, `stars-yellow-pair.png`: yellow sparkle and star clusters.
- `cloud-white-3d.png`: white 3D clouds on Activity Time and Break Time, often bleeding off the edge.
- `question-mark-pink-3d.png`: the pink 3D question mark on the "How?!" slide (+8°).
- `shooting-star-white-red.png`: the shooting star at the left of THANKS!.

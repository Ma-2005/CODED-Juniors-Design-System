# StepScreenshotSlide

One tool step: numbered actions on the left, the screenshot in a dark frame, the mentor-gate pill below.

A full 1920×1080 slide layout in the CODED Juniors camp-deck style. Copy it from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck (slide id `stepshot`), or take `slide.html` in this folder (Slides format) and copy the pictures it uses into your deck as assets: in this repo the image paths point to the `assets/` folder.

**Logo.** Shown with the solo CODED Juniors logo (203×80). For a partner program swap in the partner lockup (439×64, `lockup-juniors-kfh-*`): see Logos and partners in the README.

## How to use it

- USE FOR: one step of a tool walkthrough. One step per slide, never more.
- HOW: paste a FRESH screenshot into the dark frame and draw a red rounded box (#FB3A3B) or arrow on the button. Keep the mentor-gate pill on every step before kids generate, build or submit.
- DO: Walk around and check every screen.
- SAY: [Step instruction]. Show your mentor before you press [Generate]!
- TIME: [2 to 3] minutes.

Replace every [bracket] with real lesson content. Keep the layout, sizes and colours; change the words, the 3D icon and the Codey pose only when the slide's job changes.

# ChallengeTimeSlide

"Challenge Time!" with the challenge in one sentence and Codey kneeling, ready.

A full 1920×1080 slide layout in the CODED Juniors camp-deck style. Copy it from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck (slide id `challenge`), or take `slide.html` in this folder (Slides format) and copy the pictures it uses into your deck as assets: in this repo the image paths point to the `assets/` folder.

**Logo.** Shown with the solo CODED Juniors logo (203×80). For a partner program swap in the partner lockup (439×64, `lockup-juniors-kfh-*`): see Logos and partners in the README.

## How to use it

- USE FOR: the challenge after an activity, or the fast-finisher task.
- HOW: make it measurable for older kids ("Move a cube one meter!", "Use 10 shapes or more") and story-framed for younger ones ("Dash from Danger!"). Add "Extra points:" for a bonus.
- SAY: Finished? Here is your challenge: [challenge].
- TIME: [until the next block].

Replace every [bracket] with real lesson content. Keep the layout, sizes and colours; change the words, the 3D icon and the Codey pose only when the slide's job changes.

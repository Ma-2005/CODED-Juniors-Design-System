# CompareSlide

Two cards side by side: Weak vs Strong prompt, 2D vs 3D, With AI vs Without AI.

A full 1920×1080 slide layout in the CODED Juniors camp-deck style. Copy it from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck (slide id `compare`), or take `slide.html` in this folder (Slides format) and copy the pictures it uses into your deck as assets: in this repo the image paths point to the `assets/` folder.

**Logo.** Shown with the solo CODED Juniors logo (203×80). For a partner program swap in the partner lockup (439×64, `lockup-juniors-kfh-*`): see Logos and partners in the README.

## How to use it

- USE FOR: any side-by-side comparison: Weak vs Strong prompt, 2D vs 3D, AI Prompt vs Human Prompt, With AI vs Without AI.
- HOW: rename the pills, write both versions, drop in the two result images.
- ASK: What did the strong one say that the weak one didn't?
- TIME: 2 minutes.

Replace every [bracket] with real lesson content. Keep the layout, sizes and colours; change the words, the 3D icon and the Codey pose only when the slide's job changes.

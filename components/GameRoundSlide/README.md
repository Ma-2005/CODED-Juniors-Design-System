# GameRoundSlide

One ice-breaker or quiz round: two picture cards whose answer pills pop in on click.

A full 1920×1080 slide layout in the CODED Juniors camp-deck style. Copy it from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck (slide id `round`), or take `slide.html` in this folder (Slides format) and copy the pictures it uses into your deck as assets: in this repo the image paths point to the `assets/` folder.

**Logo.** Shown with the solo CODED Juniors logo (203×80). For a partner program swap in the partner lockup (439×64, `lockup-juniors-kfh-*`): see Logos and partners in the README.

## How to use it

- USE FOR: one game round with two pictures (2D or 3D?, AI or Not?, Spot the Difference).
- HOW: drop a picture into each grey box. The two answer pills pop in on click, so kids guess first. Duplicate the slide for each round and change the ROUND number.
- ASK: [Question]? Hands up!
- DO: Click to show the answers.
- TIME: 1 to 2 minutes per round.

Replace every [bracket] with real lesson content. Keep the layout, sizes and colours; change the words, the 3D icon and the Codey pose only when the slide's job changes.

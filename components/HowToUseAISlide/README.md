# HowToUseAISlide

The fixed "How to Use AI!" slide with the three Codey badges: Be Creative, Use AI Responsibly, Stay Safe.

A full 1920×1080 slide layout in the CODED Juniors camp-deck style. Copy it from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck (slide id `aisafety`), or take `slide.html` in this folder (Slides format) and copy the pictures it uses into your deck as assets: in this repo the image paths point to the `assets/` folder.

**Logo.** Shown with the solo CODED Juniors logo (203×80). For a partner program swap in the partner lockup (439×64, `lockup-juniors-kfh-*`): see Logos and partners in the README.

## How to use it

- USE FOR: before EVERY AI tool, in every deck. Do not skip it.
- SAY: Three rules for AI. Be creative. Be responsible. Stay safe: never type your full name, password or home address. And remember: AI is smart but not perfect. YOU are the guide!
- TIME: 1 minute.

Replace every [bracket] with real lesson content. Keep the layout, sizes and colours; change the words, the 3D icon and the Codey pose only when the slide's job changes.

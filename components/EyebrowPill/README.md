# EyebrowPill

The rounded label pills: red eyebrow pills on light slides, white pills on red, the step and answer pills.

- Eyebrow pills sit at y=150 above a heading at y=226: `juniors-red` fill, white 30px 800 uppercase, 10×28px padding.
- Status pills: `success-green` for Strong/answers, `ink-faint` for Weak, `keyword-amber` for "Think about…".
- On red: white pill, red text.

# YouAreASlide

The identity hook "You Are a [Designer] Today!", split with a red panel and Codey running.

A full 1920×1080 slide layout in the CODED Juniors camp-deck style. Copy it from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck (slide id `mission`), or take `slide.html` in this folder (Slides format) and copy the pictures it uses into your deck as assets: in this repo the image paths point to the `assets/` folder.

**Logo.** Shown with the solo CODED Juniors logo (203×80). For a partner program swap in the partner lockup (439×64, `lockup-juniors-kfh-*`): see Logos and partners in the README.

## How to use it

- USE FOR: the identity hook after the ice breaker. Kids should feel like the expert today.
- ROLE IDEAS: Designer, Game Developer, Engineer, Innovator, Cyber Hero, Entrepreneur, Super Coder.
- SAY: Today YOU are a [role], just like the people who [what real ones do].
- TIME: 30 seconds.

Replace every [bracket] with real lesson content. Keep the layout, sizes and colours; change the words, the 3D icon and the Codey pose only when the slide's job changes.

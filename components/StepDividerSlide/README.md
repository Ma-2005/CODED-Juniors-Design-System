# StepDividerSlide

The big step divider: "Step 1" in yellow over "Designing!" in white.

A full 1920×1080 slide layout in the CODED Juniors camp-deck style. Copy it from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck (slide id `step`), or take `slide.html` in this folder (Slides format) and copy the pictures it uses into your deck as assets: in this repo the image paths point to the `assets/` folder.

**Logo.** Shown with the solo CODED Juniors logo (203×80). For a partner program swap in the partner lockup (439×64, `lockup-juniors-kfh-*`): see Logos and partners in the README.

## How to use it

- USE FOR: the big step dividers of the activity. The house pattern: Step 1 Designing! → Step 2 Innovating! (or Coding!) → Step 3 [Print in Action! / Share!]. Day 2 carries on the numbering.
- HOW: keep the step word to one or two short words, so it stays on one line.
- SAY: Step [1] is [Designing]. First, let's learn [topic].

Replace every [bracket] with real lesson content. Keep the layout, sizes and colours; change the words, the 3D icon and the Codey pose only when the slide's job changes.

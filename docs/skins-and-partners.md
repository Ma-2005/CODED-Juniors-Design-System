# Skins and partners

One system, a few skins. A skin changes the hero colour, the cover art and the logo; layouts, type, keywords, Codey and the rules stay the same.

| Line | Hero colour | Cover and title | Logo |

## Partner rules
- A partner logo appears only on that partner's program.
- The lockup is one image: CODED Juniors, a thin divider, the partner. Don't rebuild it from two logos.
- KFH: current star logo only, white on red/blue/navy, green on white/paper/yellow.
- KNET, Senan and other partners: their logo top-right on the opening slides only, unless the partner asks for every slide.

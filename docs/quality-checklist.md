# Quality checklist

Run this on every deck before it goes to an instructor.

## Content
- Title, day, year, program and age group are correct on the cover and title (no "Day 1" on a Day 2 deck, no old years).
- A "Today you will…" goal slide comes after the title.
- Every new idea gets a question slide, then a definition slide.
- "How to Use AI!" comes before every AI tool; the mentor gate is on every generate, build or submit step.
- Every session ends with What Did We Learn + "Which Part did you Enjoy the Most?" and Thanks.
- Share (Show & Tell) is in the deck and is never cut.
- Tools named in the deck match the lesson plan.
- Spell-check pass. Known repeat typos: "Revision is Matter!", "a AI", "Heros", "Spirits" (sprites), "RasberryPi", "Lets/youre".

## Look
- Two backgrounds only: juniors-red and content-white.
- Manrope 800, Title Case, nothing under 30px.
- 1–3 coloured keywords per sentence; only white, yellow and lavender on red.
- Codey in the right pose; 3D icons, no emoji, no flat clip-art.
- Logo rule followed (solo vs partner lockup), logos clear of titles.
- Divider subtitles short enough to stay on one line ("Challeng/e" must never wrap).
- Fresh tool screenshots with red boxes on the button.
- One step per screenshot slide; link to official build guides instead of 30+ build-step slides.

## Safety and privacy
- No passwords, Wi-Fi, class codes, game PINs or shared logins on slides or in notes.
- No kids' real names, faces or IDs in screenshots; use demo data or Codey.
- Third-party characters (Mario, Sonic…) only as named demo subjects, never as brand art.

## Notes and size
- Speaker notes on every slide: SAY / ASK / DO and TIME.
- Timings add up to the slot.
- Compress videos and GIFs; the exported deck stays under 30 MB.

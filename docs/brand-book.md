CODED Juniors is the kids' branch of CODED Academy in Kuwait: camps, weekend programs, school programs and partner programs for ages 6–13, teaching coding, AI, robotics, game development and design. **This is the one CODED Juniors design system.** It records the camp-deck style used in every real Juniors deck: Juniors red and soft white, Manrope ExtraBold in Title Case, multi-coloured keywords, Codey the robot, glossy 3D icons and Arabic value stickers.

It was built from about 230 Juniors 2026 Google Slides decks (colours sampled from the rendered slides), the KFH Baiti weekend decks and the **CODED Juniors Slide Template**, a Slides deck with all 31 layouts below (claude.ai/artifact/BdYQW2CyKfHwKBMoqmGjfc). The full teaching method is in the project doc *CODED_Juniors_Curriculum_Design_Playbook.md*. The JR-25 Brand Guide (2025) pages are kept in the Brand guide asset group; its CODED_TYPE font and #FF3E3E red are for print and merch. **On slides, this system wins.** School Program (BSK) decks use the separate BSK Program system.

**Brand words:** fun first, then learning. Interactive, bright, hands-on, kid-sized, proud.

## The rules that come first

- **Two backgrounds per deck.** `juniors-red` (hero and divider slides) and `content-white` (content slides). Never navy, yellow or blue slides (skins below are the exception).
- **Manrope ExtraBold (800) for everything.** Headlines in Title Case ("What is a Prompt?"). No slide text under 30px.
- **Colour the key words.** One to three key words per sentence, in order: `keyword-1` red → `keyword-2` blue → `keyword-3` amber (→ `keyword-4` purple). The other words stay `ink`. On red slides, key words go `yellow-on-red`.
- **One idea per slide, about 20 words.** Question slide first, definition second.
- **Codey on every hero and split slide**, in the pose that matches the slide's job (Mascot below).
- **Logos never move.** Top-right on most slides, top-left on covers and on split slides where the right half is red. Never tilted, animated or covered.
- **Mentor gate before every AI step**: the pill "Ask your mentor if you're good to go!".
- **"How to Use AI!" before every AI tool** (the three Codey badges, fixed text).
- **Speaker notes on every slide**: what to SAY, ASK or DO, and the TIME.
- **Never on slides:** passwords, class codes, Wi-Fi, game PINs, kids' real names or photos. Write "Your mentor will share it!".

## Content fundamentals

**Voice.** High energy, warm and direct. "You" to the kid, "we" and "Let's…" for the class. Lots of "!": "It's YOUR Turn Now!", "Be Creative!", "HOW?!", "Let's Get Writing!". Kids are Juniors, Coders, Innovators or Designers. Adults are "your mentor" and "your instructor" on slides, and "Coach [Name]" or "Mentor [Name]" in the room. Never Mr/Ms/استاذ/ابله.

**Casing.** Title Case On Key Words. The ALL-CAPS word is a tool for emphasis ("OVER", "YOUR", "THANKS!"), one per slide.

**Teaching moves (use them).**
- Question → definition: "What do you think AI means?" → "Machines that Learn, Create, and Help us."
- One analogy per idea: filament = ink, block coding = building blocks, variable = treasure chest, credits = coins, slicing = cutting bread.
- Weak vs strong examples for prompts.
- Click-to-reveal answers in games and recaps.
- Tangible output every session; Share is never cut; end on pride.

**House phrases.** "Activity Time!", "Challenge Time!", "EXTRA Time!", "LIVE DEMO Time!", "TESTING TIME!", "BREAK Time! Let's Relax and Continue Later!", "Ask your mentor if you're good to go!", "Credits are like Coins! Careful not to OVER use them.", "Not Magic, just Trained with Data.", "Clear + Detailed = Great Result!", "What did we Learn Today?", "Which Part did you Enjoy the Most?", "THANKS! Have Fun Coding!" (design and girls programs: "Have Fun Innovating!").

**Numbers and labels.** "Day 1", "Step 2", "Round 3", "Ages 6–13", "KD 120".

**Language.** Kid slides are in English. Arabic is cultural: the value stickers, the salaam greeting in the coding chain, Kuwaiti-dialect ice breakers, and Arabic-first partner decks (Senan). Arabic text uses Vazirmatn.

## Visual foundations

**Colour.** `juniors-red` #FB3A3B is the brand field. `content-white` #F6F6F6 is the content ground, with `card-white` cards and `ink` #1A1A1A text. The keyword colours are `keyword-blue` #2474BA, `keyword-amber` #E8890C and `keyword-purple` #7C3AED, plus `success-green` for right answers. On red, use only white, `yellow-on-red` #FFD15C and `lavender-on-red` #DCD3FF. Contrast: white on red is 3.6:1, so text on red is always bold and 44px or more. Amber (2.4:1) and the yellow and lavender on red (about 2.5:1) are the decks' own pairings, kept for big display words only. Never use them for body text.

**Type.** One family, Manrope (Google Fonts), at weight 800 for titles and 700 for body. Scale on a 1920×1080 slide: dividers 180 · hero titles 150 · statements 120 · headings and definitions 84 · subtitles 64 · lists 54 · body 48 · cards 40 · pills 30–34 · minimum 30.

**Layout.** Content starts 120px from the left and right edges. Headings sit at y=150, or at y=226 under an eyebrow pill. A 2px rule runs across the bottom, 70px up. Split slides give one side (845px) to red. Cards are white, 32px corners, with `shadow-card`. Pills are fully round.

**The 8 layout families** (each is a slide component):
1. **Hero on red**: title left, Codey right (Title, Ice Breaker, Challenge, Break, Game).
2. **Centred divider**: two huge lines on red, decor around (Step, Activity Time, Thanks).
3. **Split**: red half with a big word and Codey or a 3D icon, white half with the sentence (Definition, Fun Fact, Credits, Mission, Coming Up Next, Tool reveal).
4. **Card row**: heading plus 2–4 white cards (Three Cards, Compare, How to Use AI, Rules, Recap).
5. **List with icons**: heading plus lines, each led by a 3D icon or star (Recap Stars, What Did We Learn).
6. **Screenshot step**: steps on the left, dark screenshot frame on the right, mentor gate at the bottom.
7. **Grid**: 4×2 picture tiles (Pick Your Style).
8. **Template box**: the dashed red prompt box with coloured [BLANKS].

**Mascot: Codey.** A cream 3D robot with a black TV-screen face, cyan LED smile, red ear discs and a red cape. Use the pose by job: laptop = cover · hands on hips = activity title · bursting through a wall = Ice Breaker · running = "You Are a…" · thinking = every question · lightbulb = Fun Fact · kneeling = Challenge · jumping = Game Time · sunglasses + lemonade = Break · hello bubble = Show & Tell · reading = What Did We Learn · waving = Coming Up Next. One Codey per slide, big (500–800px).

**3D icons.** Glossy clay-style 3D icons are the only illustration style: one or two per slide, big (190–400px), sometimes tilted. No flat clip-art. **No emoji on slides**: the 3D icons do that job.

**Stickers and decor.** Arabic value stickers (إبتكار · برمجة · بطل · بطلة · إبداع · وناسة) go tilted around the Thanks slide. Use the 3D yellow star, white sparkles, white clouds and the pink 3D question mark as decor, two to four per hero slide. The Super Coder badge goes on Activity Time.

**Shadows and tilt.** Soft shadows only (`shadow-card`, `shadow-tile-on-red`). Stickers, stars and date tiles tilt; text never does.

**Motion.** Answers and revealed steps pop in on click (build-in "pop"). Slides fade. Nothing loops behind text.

## Logos and partners

- **No partner:** the CODED Juniors winged logo alone. Use `juniors-logo-white` on red and `juniors-logo-red` on light, 203×80px.
- **With a partner (for example KFH Baiti, or KFH-sponsored Girls Camp):** the partner lockup, CODED Juniors | divider | partner, at 439×64px. Use `lockup-juniors-kfh-white` on red and `lockup-juniors-kfh-color` on light. KFH is white on red and green on light. Never the old KFH logo, and never a separate CODED box logo.
- **Winter Camp only:** `juniors-logo-winter` (the logo with snow).
- **White-label partners (Boubyan Al-Ghaly):** no CODED or Juniors wording, partner skin only.
- A tool's logo (Gemini, Meshy, Tinkercad…) appears only where that tool is taught.

## Iconography

3D icon PNGs only (Icons3D group); the ✦ four-point star is the only glyph used as a spark. For a UI glyph in documents or web, use Lucide at stroke 2.25 with round caps.

## Building a deck with this system

1. Read the slide-templates section (session flow) and the quality checklist.
2. Start from the **CODED Juniors Slide Template** deck, or copy a component's `slide.html` (Slides format, in its folder). Copy the pictures it uses into the deck as assets; the `/_blob/` ids in `slide.html` belong to this system.
3. Pick the right logo rule, colour the keywords, put Codey in the right pose, and write speaker notes.
4. More pictures: the CODED Juniors Asset Library folder (about 500 images: 55 Codey poses, 205 3D icons, hardware, tool logos, backgrounds).
